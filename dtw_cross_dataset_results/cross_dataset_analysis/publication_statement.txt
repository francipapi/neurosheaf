DTW ROBUSTNESS STATEMENT FOR PUBLICATION:

Our DTW fingerprint configuration demonstrates robust stability across multiple neural network datasets. Testing on both Dataset 1 (N=Unknown (from backup)) and Dataset 2 (Digits Hourglass, N=40) with 14 parameter configurations within reasonable ranges (window ∈ [0.05, 0.2], smooth_win ∈ [10, 25], resample ∈ [150, 250]):

- Cross-dataset consistency: Maximum ARI difference ≤ 1.026, Maximum Silhouette difference ≤ 0.068
- Within-dataset robustness: Matrix Spearman ρ ≥ 0.451 across both datasets

These results validate that our DTW configuration generalizes robustly across different neural architectures and training paradigms.
